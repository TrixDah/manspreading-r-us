# Do Geo Results Need To Be Waved?

Following the 3/4 VCE Geography Exams done in the past few days, some students have proudly boasted high marks, while others are forced to sit with an underwhelming performance.

One student, however, was particularly vocal when regarding what exactly went wrong for them, following a failed exam.

The student, rather than accept that they may have dropped the ball when studying and preparing, went after their teachers and classes, claiming that a lack of flag and geography quizzes let them down.

I was there on the scene to catch interviews, here's what our student, who has chosen to remain anonymous, had to say.

> "It's an injustice really. They expect us to enjoy this subject and perform well, but the lack of flag quizzes in class really let us all down, and the class quickly became an uninteresting drag. I took this class to flex my flag knowledge I got from playing FIFA all these years, not why longshore drift is effecting erosion!"

The student, who was joined by his friends after the exam, then stormed off with a fairly displeased look on his face.  After some more interviews, I grasped the idea that this outraged student is essentially an 'odd one out', yet that doesn't make his claims any less relevant and discussion-worthy.

The student then made a remarkable return, parading around the flag of Gabon. When asked why, he said:

> "Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang bro, if you know you know!"

Following this bold, yet courageous outburst, the student was escorted away and sent home.

So, now we are left with the commanding conundrum, will schools change their methods? Or are more students going to be left hanging dry after being let down by the course content?
