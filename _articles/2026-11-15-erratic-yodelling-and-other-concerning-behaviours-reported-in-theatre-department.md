After [supporting a raise in canteen prices to finance a new robot dog](https://mentonemanspreaders.com/articles/robotdog.html) and [deploying their AI Brainy Bot to lead polls for the upcoming English Head election](https://mentonemanspreaders.com/articles/2026-10-27-copy-and-pasting-ai-answers-is-harder-than-teaching-reports-ai-dependent-teacher.html), IT has now involved itself in a third aspect of school life.

Mentone Manspreaders arrived at the drama rooms at 9:22 am, 12:07 pm for our ever loyal Chatham Island readers, after receiving reports of erratic yodelling by multiple eyewitnesses. Upon entering the classroom, it was clear something major had happened. Some were laid dramatically across chairs, others wept profusely, but all possessed a thousand-yard stare.

Attempts by our team to communicate with the Theatre Kids were unsuccessful. It was clear whatever occurred had reduced their capability to communicate down to their first language, Hamiltonish, a mix of drama games and dancing. Further attempts eventually led one Theatre Kid to dance, seemingly trying to communicate.

After texting and waiting for the local interpretive dance translator to arrive, the dancer’s message soon became clear. The Theatre Department had announced a collaboration with IT, using AI for official communications (including an AI-written Audition Information pack for next year’s play) and even for modifying the play’s script. 

Using a mix of shimmying and jazz hands, they articulated the general mood of the Theatre Kids:

> “AI is framed as a solution to the inconveniences of life. If we automate the struggle most fundamental to the human condition, the creative struggle, what’s left?”

They then proceeded to moon walk to emphasise their point. Reactions to the news varied across the College. Brainy Bot, the leading candidate for English Head, welcomed the news, calling for the establishment of a drama-specialised agentic AI, called Acting Automaton. 

Students rights groups criticised the lack of student consultation in the decision, supporting the stance of the theatre kids, and proposing that students could be the ones modifying the script.

This event occurs in a broader backdrop of a college in civil war. The spread and adoption of AI, zealously pushed by IT and Cybermorgs as the future, with more Traditionalist-aligned groups pushing back against recent Cybermorg victories, labelling them as examples of the “tyranny of technology” and questioning the power IT has by controlling and monitoring both the school’s communication platforms and artificial intelligence.

The most recent setback for Cybermorgs occurred when an AI agent was teaching an English class, playing Terminator on the projector. The sight of Arnold Schwarzenegger topless was so hot that the AI exploded.

Both sides of the civil war seem unwilling to step down. Whatever the result, one can always rely on the Theatre Kids being dramatic.
