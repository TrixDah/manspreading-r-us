Unfortunate Student Evaporates as School Air Conditioning Fails to Deliver Once Again

A Year 10 student has reportedly turned into water vapour after overheating in a Religion lesson, prompting fresh calls for the College to fund and maintain its air-conditioning units.

By Staff Reporter · 23 October 2026 · 3 min read

There have been fresh calls for an increase in funding and maintenance for air-conditioning units across the College after a Year 10 student allegedly melted into a puddle and then evaporated into water vapour during one particularly boiling Religion lesson in the F building.

Reports suggest the room reached roughly 5,500°C — supposedly equivalent to the surface temperature of the Sun, or about half the temperature of seatbelts and concrete on a hot day. Forensic investigation by Mentone Manspreaders, consisting of a conversation with the maintenance crew who fixed the unit, reveals that the room became so warm the air conditioner simply decided to give up.

The incident follows a broader trend across the College. Advocates say many rooms in the F, S and M buildings, also known as the Senior classrooms, are older and less equipped to deal with Australia's increasingly hot summers.

The College responded to our request for comment by saying that student satisfaction and learning environments remain its top priorities, pointing Manspreaders towards the $1.95 million upgrades to both the Middle Years and Senior buildings.
