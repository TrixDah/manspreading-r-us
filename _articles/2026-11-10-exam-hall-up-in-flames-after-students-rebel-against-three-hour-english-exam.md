# Rage Reins Supreme as Anarchy and Mayhem Erupts in Exam Hall

What started as a run of the mill average Year 12 English examination, quickly turned hostile and chaotic. After being told that the exam runs for three hours, students erupted into displeased jeers and hollers.

Although teachers and staff were able to suffocate intial movements, the students only grew more aggrivated as time went on. By 30 minutes in, they were up out of their seats, yelling for humane justice.

Staff quickly realised they were outnumbered, and therfore decided to step away. Students ran wild, tearing papers, stacking desks and crafting flags and posters on the fly with exam papers.

Then, pure, unbridled rage and chaos reigned. Students ran off school grounds, and returned with smuggled flares. Following intial confusion on how to operate it, flares soon went up inside the exam hall.

Unfortnuately, things escelated further after a flare was tossed into sheets of paper. Flames erupted through the room, cascading down the walls. The fire department was soon called into action, although the students seemed unbothered, almost enjoying the arsonic activity.

I was there after the events, interviewing singed students who were being escorted out as the fire brigade took over.

> "This is pure mentality, you can bottle it up and sell it. We have had enough! Prosecute us, arrest us, we don't care anymore!"

Passionate words from a passionate student. The whole incident is being swept essentially under the rug, with little media coverage and chance for serious punishment. Zero casulaties and injuries mean this was a standard raising sort of peaceful protest.
