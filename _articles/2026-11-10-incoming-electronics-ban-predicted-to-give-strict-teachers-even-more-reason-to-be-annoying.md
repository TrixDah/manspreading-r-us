## There's No Place Like School

The Victorian Government has recently passed a law banning headphones, phones, and Apple watches in non-government schools, bringing them in line with government schools. Reactions in the College have been sharply divided, with students calling it a dramatic overreach, while many teachers have praised it. When the Education Minister was asked by a reporter from *Mentone Manspreaders* about the reasoning behind the ban, they responded: 

>  "We want students to be immersed in the real world, a world where the education system has been severely underfunded by the government for decades, and where overburdened teachers try and cling to the faintest shred of sanity."

The College's Strict Teachers Council has also put out a statement in support of the proposal, arguing that the law allows them to be an even bigger nuisance and drag for students. In response the College's Well-Adjusted Human Beings Council fought back, proposing that the only reason strict teachers are so controlling in the first place is due to a lack of control in other areas of their life.

Such technological restrictions in the College are not unheard of, with previous bans on using phones to buy things from the Canteen being a potential factor in the Canteen rising prices. There is also a blanket ban on phones from Year 7-12, though teachers are more than happy to embrace AI generated content, with reports some teachers generate the majority of their class materials on such tools.
