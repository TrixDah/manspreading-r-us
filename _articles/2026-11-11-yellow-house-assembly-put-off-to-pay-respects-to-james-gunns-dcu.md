# The Gunn Has Jammed, Causing Yellow House to Cancel Weekly Assembly Out of Respect

Every second Wednesday morning usually brings about a standard assembly for Yellow house, giving a chance to summarise the weeks' events and what to look forward too.

However, this was thrown in flux after James Gunn, formerly CEO of DC Studios, was fired on Tuesday. Following the devestating news, many students reportedly asked for days off to commemorate his work over the last four years.

Yellow house and it's glorious leaders recognised the delicate situation, and decided that the annual Wednesday assembly needed to take a back seat in this emotional time.

Students have spoken out about how well the situation has been handled.
