# Rage Reigns Supreme as Anarchy and Mayhem Erupt in Exam Hall

What started as a run-of-the-mill Year 12 English examination quickly turned hostile and chaotic. After being told that the exam would run for three hours, examinees erupted into displeased jeers and hollers.

Although teachers and staff were able to suppress initial movements, the disgruntled cohort only grew more aggravated as time went on. By 30 minutes in, they were up out of their seats, yelling for humane justice.

Staff quickly realised they were outnumbered and therefore decided to step away. The protesters ran wild, tearing papers, stacking desks and crafting flags and posters on the fly using exam papers.

Then, pure, unbridled rage and chaos reigned. Demonstrators ran off school grounds and returned with smuggled flares. Following initial confusion about how to operate them, flares soon went up inside the exam hall.

Unfortunately, things escalated further after a flare was tossed into sheets of paper. Flames erupted through the room, cascading down the walls. The fire department was soon called into action, although the crowd seemed unbothered, almost enjoying the arsonic activity.

I was there after the events, interviewing singed examinees who were being escorted out as the fire brigade took over.

> "This is pure mentality, you can bottle it up and sell it. We have had enough! Prosecute us, arrest us, we don't care anymore!"

Passionate words from a passionate protester. The whole incident is essentially being swept under the rug, with little media coverage and little chance of serious punishment. Zero casualties and no injuries mean this was a standard-raising sort of peaceful protest.
